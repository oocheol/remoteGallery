## Artwork presentation updates
`PATCH /api/galleries/:id` accepts optional `artworkStyles: [{id,frameMaterial,frameWidthMm,frameDepthMm,matWidthMm,matTopMm?,matBottomMm?}]` together with `scene`, `placements`, and `expectedRevision`. The revision is mandatory when styles are supplied. Materials are wood/black; dimensions are finite numbers in 0–1000mm. Styles must reference unique artworks in this gallery. Style changes, scene and placements are validated and saved in one transaction. Failed validation changes neither styles nor revision. Older records default to black material and zero mat; zero frame width means no visible frame. Frame and mat occupy the entered outer dimensions: visible image width = widthMm minus twice frameWidthMm and twice matWidthMm; visible image height = heightMm minus twice frameWidthMm, matTopMm and matBottomMm; external dimensions equal widthMm/heightMm. Their combined inset must leave at least 1mm of image on each axis. All geometry, bounds and collision calculations share this layout. Current artwork creation/style updates and new shares use matSizing=inset. Old shares lacking matSizing are returned as outset to preserve their original presentation; the renderer retains that legacy mode only for compatibility.

## Observers

`Scene.observers?: {id:string, placementId:string, heightMm:number}[]` stores up to 100 observers, one per placement, with height 500–2500mm. Geometry derives the observer's eye position one horizontal metre from the artwork front surface and estimates eye height as 93% of height. Positions follow placements; bodies stay grounded at floor.y. Scene PATCH validates unique IDs and placement references and requires expectedRevision when observers change. Exhibition PUT removes observers linked to deleted placements. Immutable shares include observers in their scene snapshot.


## Fixed outer artwork size

`widthMm` and `heightMm` include the frame. `artworkLayout` subtracts twice the frame width, equal side mats and independent top/bottom mats to derive photo dimensions; geometry, collision checks and placement extents use the entered outer size. Combined frame and mat must leave at least 1mm of image in each dimension. `frameSizing` defaults to `inset` for editor artwork and is explicit on new shares. Old immutable share responses supply `frameSizing: outset` when absent, preserving their original frame growth alongside their existing `matSizing` semantics.


## Independent vertical mat

`matWidthMm` is the equal left/right inset; `matTopMm` and `matBottomMm` are optional independent vertical insets (0–1000mm). Missing top/bottom fields fall back to matWidthMm, preserving older records/shares. Style requests that omit these fields retain the old uniform-mat request convention. Editor saves explicitly send all three values. Horizontal validity: 2×frame + 2×side ≤ width−1. Vertical validity: 2×frame + top + bottom ≤ height−1. In Y-up rendering, the photo center offset is (bottom−top)/2 mm inside the artwork's rotated group; the SVG starts at frame+top from its upper edge. Frame outer dimensions and placement coordinates stay unchanged.
